# SarlaYash Mission — Powered By Kapil
## AGENTIC AI SOFTWARE TESTING USING AGILE TESTING PROCESS
### Real-Time ERP Product Testing with Agentic AI

An enterprise-grade, interactive learning academy and hands-on IDE engineered for QA Engineers, SDETs, Test Leads, and engineering graduates to master **Agentic AI** integrated into the complete Software Testing Life Cycle (STLC) for real-time ERP products (Order-to-Cash and Procure-to-Pay).

---

## 🌟 Key Capabilities & Features

1. **Interactive Real Practice IDE**:
   - **Multi-Agent Testing Workflows**: Pre-configured with **CrewAI** (Requirement Analyst, Scenario Designer, Lead Reviewer), **AutoGen** (Conversational 3-Way Matching Bug Triage), and **LangGraph** (Build Release Verification & Quality Gate graph).
   - **Functional Automation**: Pytest + Playwright executable suites for ERP Order-to-Cash credit and discount validation.
   - **Prompt Engineering Playground**: Context Engineering and System Persona testing against enterprise SRS specs.
   - **Automated RTM Engine**: Live matrix generator mapping 100% of user stories to test cases and Jira defect keys.
   - **Execution Console & Metrics**: Step-by-step agent thinking, action-observation logs, pass/fail counts, and deliverable export.

2. **Google Sign-In / Account Authentication**:
   - Google SSO integration with multi-account selector and custom Gmail support.
   - Candidate ID, learning streak tracker, XP counter, and profile management.
   - Persistent storage synchronization across sessions.

3. **Complete 14 Modules across 10 Mastery Levels**:
   - **Level 1 (Module 1)**: QA Expectations & ERP Architecture (BRD, SRS, FSD, Context Engineering)
   - **Level 2 (Module 2)**: Requirement Analysis Using Agentic AI (SRS Decomposition, Gap Analysis)
   - **Level 3 (Module 3)**: Test Scenario Design & AI Peer Review Workflow
   - **Level 4 (Modules 4 & 5)**: Requirement Traceability Matrix (RTM) & Automated Test Case Design
   - **Level 5 (Modules 6 & 7)**: Build Release 1 Management & Automated Smoke Gate
   - **Level 6 (Module 8)**: Functional Test Execution & Live Dynamic RTM
   - **Level 7 (Module 9)**: Defect Management, AI Duplicate Detection & Root Cause Analysis (Jira)
   - **Level 8 (Modules 10 & 11)**: QA Reporting (DSR/DDR/WSR) & Build 2 Re-Testing
   - **Level 9 (Module 12)**: Regression Testing & AI Impact Analysis
   - **Level 10 (Modules 13 & 14)**: Advanced QA Dashboards, Exit Criteria & Final Test Closure

4. **Level Mock Assessments (Timed)**:
   - Module-level scenario quizzes with countdown timers, immediate explanations, and XP rewards.

5. **30-Minute Final Capstone Certification Exam**:
   - Comprehensive 20-question exam covering all 14 modules, STLC rigor, and ERP test logic.
   - Interactive question palette, flagging, countdown timer, and passing threshold (80%+).

6. **Badges System (12+ Unlockable Achievements)**:
   - Visual badges: *QA Vanguard, SRS Investigator, Scenario Architect, Traceability Maestro, Defect Hunter, Certified Agentic QA Lead, etc.*

7. **Official Verifiable Certificate of Completion**:
   - Conferred under **SarlaYash Mission Powered By Kapil**.
   - Features Candidate Name, Course Title, Unique Verification ID, QR Code, Gold Seal, and instructor signatures.
   - Full **PDF download, PNG export, and 1-click Print** capabilities.

8. **Real-Time ERP Sandbox**:
   - Interactive Order-to-Cash ($50K approval threshold, credit hold rules) and Procure-to-Pay (3-way match variance) simulators.
